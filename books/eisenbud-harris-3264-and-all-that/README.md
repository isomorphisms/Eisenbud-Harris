# 3264 and All That

**David Eisenbud and Joe Harris. *3264 and All That: A Second Course in Algebraic Geometry*. Cambridge University Press, 2016.**

Publisher/DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139062046

## Role here

This is the concrete intersection-theory companion. Its path through Chow rings, Grassmannians, Chern classes, projective bundles, Segre classes, Porteous' formula, excess intersections, blow-ups, and Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch should be cross-read against Fulton rather than summarized in isolation.

Cambridge's contents make the bridge unusually explicit: the book starts with the Chow ring and builds toward excess intersection and GRR through enumerative problems.
