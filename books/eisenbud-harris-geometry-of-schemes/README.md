## Role here

This is the direct bridge from the commutative-algebra spine to scheme language and geometric constructions. Keep explicit backlinks to the algebraic constructions that schemes package geometrically.

The useful questions for notes are concrete: which ring/module operation is being geometrized, what local data it retains, and what becomes easier to state or prove after passing to schemes.
