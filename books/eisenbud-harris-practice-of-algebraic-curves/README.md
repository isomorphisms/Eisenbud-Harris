# The Practice of Algebraic Curves

**David Eisenbud and Joe Harris. *The Practice of Algebraic Curves: A Second Course in Algebraic Geometry*. Graduate Studies in Mathematics 250, American Mathematical Society, 2024.**

AMS: https://bookstore.ams.org/gsm-250

## Role here

This is the modern curve-theory continuation of the Eisenbud–Harris line. The AMS describes the book as ranging across classical geometry, modern commutative algebra, and moduli theory, including Jacobians, Hilbert schemes, Brill–Noether theory, free resolutions, canonical curves, and Hilbert schemes of curves.

The AMS has made an authorized eBook freely available. Link to that edition rather than copying it into this repository.
