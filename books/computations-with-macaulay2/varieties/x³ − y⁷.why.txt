This is the second generator of the opening curve ideal. Taken alone it is another hypersurface in affine three-space. Imposed simultaneously with x⁴ − y⁵, it helps cut the ambient three-space down to the object whose dimension, codimension, degree, saturation, and radical the chapter investigates.

The generator remains separate from the resulting curve because a system is not the same mathematical object as any single defining polynomial.
