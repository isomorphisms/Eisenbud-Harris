The opening computation starts with two equations in QQ[x,y,z]. This is the first generator.

On its own, x⁴ − y⁵ = 0 cuts out a hypersurface in affine three-space; z remains free. The book immediately pairs it with x³ − y⁷ so the ideal carries more structure than either equation alone.

Keeping this polynomial separate matters because later operations act on the ideal generated by both equations, while SURFER can render the individual hypersurface. The pair distinguishes one equation, a system, and the geometry produced by the system.
