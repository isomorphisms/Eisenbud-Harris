Kind: matrix / hypersurface seed
Matrix U:
[ w  x ]
[ y  z ]
Hypersurface polynomial: f = x×y − w×z = −det(U)
Nullhomotopy produces V with U×V = V×U = −f.
Source: completeIntersections/chapter.m2 lines 1–9; test.m2 lines 2–10.
Why: This is the basic matrix-factorization example: the cokernel of U is annihilated by the determinant hypersurface, and the adjugate-like partner V realizes the factorization.
