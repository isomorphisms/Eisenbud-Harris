# Why this variety appears

This object is the geometric intersection the section is aiming at. At the ideal level the construction is a **sum**: every generator from the curve and every generator from the surface is imposed simultaneously.

The following computations ask for its dimension, degree, degree after taking the radical, and the leading-term ideal used to obtain a monomial staircase. That makes this object a useful bridge from a visible geometric intersection to computational invariants.

Do not replace this system by a product polynomial; that would encode a union instead.
