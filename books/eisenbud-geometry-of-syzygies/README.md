# The Geometry of Syzygies

**David Eisenbud. Graduate Texts in Mathematics 229, Springer, 2005.**

Publisher/DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/b137572

## Role here

Follow free resolutions out of *Commutative Algebra* into projective geometry. This book makes the equations of a variety and the relations among those equations into geometric data.

Track at least: free resolutions, Hilbert functions, Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity, projective curves, linear series, canonical embeddings, and the computational side of resolutions.
