# Sources

Authoritative or bibliographically useful entry points for the reading project.

## Core

- David Eisenbud, *Commutative Algebra: with a View Toward Algebraic Geometry*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 150, Springer, 1995. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-5350-1
- David Eisenbud and Joe Harris, *The Geometry of Schemes*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 197, Springer, 2000. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/b97680
- David Eisenbud, *The Geometry of Syzygies: A Second Course in Algebraic Geometry and Commutative Algebra*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 229, Springer, 2005. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/b137572
- David Eisenbud and Joe Harris, *3264 and All That: A Second Course in Algebraic Geometry*, Cambridge University Press, 2016. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139062046
- William Fulton, *Intersection Theory*, 2nd ed., Springer, 1998. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-1700-8

## Wider Eisenbud–Harris orbit

- Joe Harris, *Algebraic Geometry: A First Course*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 133, Springer, 1992. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4757-2189-8
- Joe Harris and David Eisenbud, *Curves in Projective Space*, Presses de l'Université de Montréal, 1982.
- David Eisenbud and Joe Harris, *The Practice of Algebraic Curves: A Second Course in Algebraic Geometry*, Graduate Studies in Mathematics 250, American Mathematical Society, 2024. The AMS has made an authorized eBook freely available as part of a pilot program: https://bookstore.ams.org/gsm-250
- William Fulton and Joe Harris, *Representation Theory: A First Course*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 129, Springer, 1991. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-0979-9
- David Eisenbud, Daniel R. Grayson, Michael Stillman, and Bernd Sturmfels (eds.), *Computations in Algebraic Geometry with Macaulay 2*, Algorithms and Computation in Mathematics 8, Springer, 2002. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-04851-1
